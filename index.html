    About the data and how shade is modeled

    Sun. The sun's height and direction are calculated for West Lafayette from the date and time you choose (Eastern time, with daylight saving).

    Buildings. Real outlines from OpenStreetMap. Each one is treated as a block of its height, and its shadow is the exact shape that block casts for the current sun.

    Trees. Every tree sits at its mapped location. Heights and crown sizes are invented because the map doesn't record them. Trees are treated as deciduous: full leaves from May through mid-October, thinning into November, and bare from late November through March.

    Walking. Routes follow the mapped sidewalks, crosswalks, campus walks, stairs and streets. Covered walkways, tunnels and corridors count as shaded. Each stretch is checked for shade every 6 m, and each sunlit meter costs up to 5 times a shaded one (set with the Shortest ↔ Shadiest slider). Streets with separate sidewalks cost slightly more, so routes prefer the sidewalk.
