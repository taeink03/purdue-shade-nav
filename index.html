    About the data and how shade is modeled

    Sun. The sun's height and direction are calculated for West Lafayette from the date and time you choose (Eastern time, with daylight saving).

    Shadows. simulated trees and 20 simplified building footprints each cast a shadow whose length and direction follow the sun. Tree positions, crown sizes, footprints and heights are invented for this prototype; the Bell Tower uses its real 160 ft height.

    Paths. approximate the main streets and malls. Every stretch is checked for shade every 5 m.

    Routing. Dijkstra's algorithm, where each sunlit meter costs up to 5 times a shaded one (set with the Shortest ↔ Shadiest slider). Seek sun flips the penalty for cold days.

    Making it real. Swap in a campus tree inventory, GIS building footprints with heights, and the actual sidewalk network. Paste tree data below to try it.
